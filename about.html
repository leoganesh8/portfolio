About

The person behind G1 Growth.

My Story

In January 2026, I stepped into a Digital Marketing Executive role at a women's fashion e-commerce brand and its wholesale counterpart — and ended up owning the entire marketing operation myself: content, paid ads, SEO, Shopify, and Google Business Profile. It's a scope that's usually split across a team of four to five specialists, but I built my process to run it end to end, from scripting a reel in the morning to fixing product SEO in the afternoon.

Along the way I picked up every tool I needed on the job — Canva, Photoshop, CapCut, Meta & Google Ads Manager, and AI-assisted content generation — largely self-taught, because the work didn't wait for a course to finish.

What Makes Me Different

Marketing never stands still — there's something new to learn every single day, and that's exactly where I find my flow. I enjoy the process of building strategy as much as executing it — figuring out what will actually move the needle for a brand, then seeing it through myself, from the first script to the final published post.

I don't see this as a role I've settled into — I see it as a foundation. The plan is to keep building toward running my own marketing agency, and every brand I grow along the way is practice for that.
